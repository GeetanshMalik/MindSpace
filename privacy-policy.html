Privacy Policy

Mindspace is an independent wellness project designed to keep personal reflection private and to use only the information needed for the app to work.

Last Updated: May 13, 2026

1 Information We Collect

Mindspace does not collect personal information for advertising, marketing, or data selling. If you create an account or use community, chat, journal, mood, notification, or feedback features, the app may save the details you choose to enter so those features can work.

2 How We Use Your Data

Your information is used only to run the app features you request, such as signing in, saving reflections, showing your profile, sending messages, or delivering notifications. We do not sell user data, build advertising profiles, or use your content for unrelated research.

3 Data Storage & Security

Private diary and mood content is intended to stay private to your account and/or device. Account, community, chat, media, and notification data may be stored with services such as Firebase, Cloudinary, Supabase, or Expo only when needed for app functionality. No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, but the project is built to keep data use minimal.

4 Third-Party Services

Mindspace uses services such as Firebase, Cloudinary, Supabase, Expo, Netlify, and AI providers to support sign-in, database storage, media uploads, notifications, and Sage AI replies. When you use those features, the information needed for that feature may pass through those services under their own policies.

5 Your Rights

You choose what to share. You can edit or delete supported content in the app, turn notifications off, clear local journal data, or request account deletion from Settings. If you do not want something stored, avoid entering it into shared or cloud-backed features.

6 Data Retention

Data stays only as long as needed for the feature or until you delete it where deletion controls are available. Mindspace does not keep separate marketing databases or long-term analytics archives. Some provider backups or logs may take time to expire.

7 Children's Privacy

Mindspace is not directed to children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use the app or share personal information through it.

8 Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the project changes. The latest version will be shown in the app and on the website with the updated date.
